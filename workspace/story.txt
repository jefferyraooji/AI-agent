The Last Library

On a quiet hill at the edge of the city stood the last public library. Most people no longer visited it because information had long ago moved to glowing screens and invisible clouds. But every morning, an old librarian named Mira unlocked the heavy wooden doors.

One afternoon, a curious boy named Leo wandered in while escaping the rain. He had never seen so many books in one place. Mira handed him a dusty novel and said, “Stories live differently on paper.”

Leo sat by the window and began to read. Hours passed without him noticing. The rain stopped, the sun set, and the library lights flickered on. When Leo finally looked up, he smiled and asked if he could come back tomorrow.

Mira nodded warmly. For the first time in years, the silent library felt alive again.
